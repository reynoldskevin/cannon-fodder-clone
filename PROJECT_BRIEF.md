# Game Week

Build and present a playable game by the end of Game Week. Keep it simple enough to finish — a small,
polished game beats a big, broken one. Submission must be playable and demoable in under 5 minutes.

## Criteria

- Strongest graphics and visual polish.
- Best concept and design — rewards fun over polish.
- Biggest technical stretch — 3D, Unity, multiplayer, etc.

## The Idea

I want to remake Cannon Fodder, a 2D isometric scrolling point and click shoot em up. I have the original maps with starting locations of avatars, enemy soldiers, loot chests etc and want a faithful recreation with modern graphics and animations.
